CANONICAL RESUME TEMPLATE — plaintext master
================================================================================
This file is the source of truth for CONTENT: section ordering, bullet grammar,
density, and the plaintext form an ATS actually parses. `resume-master.tex` is
the source of truth for LAYOUT.

Everything the tailoring engine generates should follow this shape unless a
tailoring decision requires deviating — and when it does, the report must say
which rule was broken and why.

Structural rules encoded below:
  * One page. Five sections. No graphics, columns, tables-as-layout, or icons.
  * Header is one line of contact info separated by " | ". No mailing address.
  * EDUCATION leads while the candidate is a student or <2 years out.
  * SKILLS sits above EXPERIENCE so the keyword block is in the top third.
  * Each experience entry: TITLE, EMPLOYER, right-aligned date range, then
    2–3 bullets. Most JD-relevant bullet first.
  * Each project entry: a descriptive title that names the CAPABILITY, then
    1–2 bullets.
  * Bullets: one claim each, ~1–2 lines, verb-first, quantified where the
    number is real.
  * Dates: "Month YYYY - Month YYYY". Present roles end in "Present".
  * Present tense for current roles, past tense for ended roles.
